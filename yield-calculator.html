Crop Yield Estimate Calculator

Paddock sampling method for early grain yield estimation, based on the Agriculture Victoria guide

Walk to several representative spots in the paddock (avoiding edges, tramlines and obviously poor or thick patches) and follow these three steps:

  1. At each site, count the number of heads (cereals) or pods (canola/pulses) within a measured 1 m² area. Average these counts across your sites — this is A.
  2. Thresh out and count the grains or seeds from at least 20 heads/pods, and average — this is B.
  3. Look up (or weigh) the weight of 100 grains/seeds for the crop, in grams — this is C.

Yield (t/ha) = (A × B × C) ÷ 10,000

Grain losses before and during harvest can be significant — the guide recommends allowing for a 5–10% loss in your final estimate. This calculator implements that method. Source: Agriculture Victoria – A brief guide to estimating crop yields.
